About the event

A day built for developers, by developers.

DevFest 2025 is Bengaluru's premier developer conference bringing together engineers, designers, and product builders under one roof. Whether you're a seasoned architect or just writing your first line of code, there's something here for you.

Expect deep-dive talks on AI, cloud infrastructure, and modern web — plus hands-on workshops where you'll ship real code. Meet the people building the tools you use every day, and leave inspired to build what's next.
